About

A career built across industries, audiences, and media.

I've run operations on Bourbon Street, stood in front of the camera as a brand spokesperson, closed SaaS deals, led a blockchain product launch as VP of Sales & Marketing, hosted 65+ educational episodes, traveled to a 6,000-acre farm in Washington to document regenerative agriculture on the blockchain, and consulted with U.S. Senate policy advisors on crypto regulation, as an outside industry voice and not in a government capacity.

Across every context, the work is the same: take something complex and make it legible to the person who needs to act on it. Running 200 people on Bourbon Street during Mardi Gras. Representing a brand on ESPN. Building positioning for a blockchain product that most of the audience has never heard of. The room changes; the craft doesn't.

I'm based in the New Orleans area. I'm Louisiana Chapter President of Stand With Crypto, a repeat guest speaker at LSU New Orleans, and I'll talk to anyone who's building something worth talking about.
