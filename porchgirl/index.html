The Look Book

Real Northshore porches from last season.

Pick the direction that feels like your house, or mix elements from a few — we'll walk through favorites from last season at your consult.

Classic Red & Green

A statement ribbon, deep green garland, a wreath, and flocked mini trees. Timeless, and always the most requested.

Elegant Gold & Bronze

Wide sheer ribbon, bronze and burgundy ornaments, pinecones, and lantern light. Upscale and a little moody.

Whimsical Candy Land

Pastel garland, oversized candy props, and a nutcracker on guard. Playful, maximalist, and a family favorite.

How It Works

From your porch to done, in six steps.

The whole process is designed to take less than fifteen minutes of your time — we handle the ladder work.

1

Browse the Look Book

Scroll last year's porches and pick the style direction that feels like your house — or tell us at your consult if you'd rather blend two.

2

Book your consult

Grab a time on our calendar and pay the $50 consult fee by Venmo or PayPal to hold your spot.

$50 · Venmo or PayPal
3

Tell us about your porch

A short form: the style(s) you like, a photo of your house, and any must-haves or must-avoids.

4

Get your estimate

We price it out from your photo and porch size and send a clear, itemized total — usually within 48 hours.

5

Confirm & schedule

Approve the estimate and pick your install date. Our calendar fills up fast in November, so early is better.

6

We install, you enjoy

Our team hangs everything on-site while you go about your day. When the season's over, it's yours to keep — nothing gets picked back up.
