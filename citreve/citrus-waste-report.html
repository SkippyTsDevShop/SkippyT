Why we're doing this

Garnish waste gets guessed. We want to count it.

There's plenty of research on food waste as a whole. Almost none of it looks at bar and catering operations, and even less at citrus and garnish specifically. Ask an operator how much of their prepped citrus goes in the trash and you'll usually get a rough percentage, not a number anyone wrote down.

This project counts it: what gets bought, what gets cut, how it's stored, and what gets tossed at close. Then we look at which practices make the biggest difference.

FAQ

Questions

Who's behind this?

CITREVE, a woman-owned company in Bluffdale, Utah, founded by Chef Nettie Frank. We work with bars, caterers, and hotels every day, and we kept hearing the same guess about garnish waste with no numbers behind it.

Are my answers anonymous?

Yes. Responses are anonymized before analysis. Results are reported in groups (by venue type, size, and region), never by individual business.

What happens to my data?

It's used only for this study. We don't sell it or share it, and we don't publish any business name without written permission. Invoices and photos are stored securely and kept confidential.

Is this a sales pitch?

No. Results are shared free with everyone who takes part. Taking the survey won't put you on a sales list.

When will results be out?

Dates are coming soon. Participants get the report first, as soon as it's published.
